Low and out of stock: pill options

Each option shows two rows from the materials list: one out of stock (aggregate, 0 tons) and one low (river sand, 4.2 tons). The stock bar and quantity stay the same in every option, so only the status marking changes.

1. Tinted pill with icon and word (recommended)

A tinted pill with a warning or stop icon and a short word. Colour, shape and words all carry the status, so it still reads in full sun. The warning icon is a triangle, the out icon is a circle with a cross.

White pill with a coloured border and coloured text. Quieter than the tinted pill, and lighter on the screen. Less contrast from a distance, so it works best when only one or two rows are flagged.

4. Pill with the action built in

The pill says what to do, not just the status. "Order 40 bags" for low stock, "Order now" for out. Tapping it starts a purchase. This takes one tap from problem to action, which suits a munshi who has to order.

No pill. A coloured stripe runs down the left edge of the row and the status is a plain coloured word under the name. Cleanest on the screen, but the word is small and the stripe is easy to miss in sun.

6. Quantity against the minimum

No status word at all. The bar shows the minimum level as a marker, and the text reads "2 tons below minimum of 6". The status is a number, so no one has to learn a label. Clear for people who read numbers well, less clear for others.

Recommendation

Option 1, with the order action from option 4 added later once purchasing is designed. It uses three signals (colour, icon, word) so nobody depends on one, and the warning and stop icons are standard enough to read without a label. Option 4 fits best once the bills and purchase screens exist, so the button has somewhere to go.
