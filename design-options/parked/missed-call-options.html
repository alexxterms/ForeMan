Missed call tab: options

Where missed calls from workers show up, and how they get confirmed. Each option is shown on the attendance screen with the new text-only status buttons. A missed call never marks someone present on its own. The supervisor always taps to confirm.

A. Dedicated tab with a count

Two tabs at the top of attendance: Crew and Missed calls. The badge shows how many are waiting. Clearest separation, one extra tap to reach them.

E. Shown on the worker's own row

No tab, no banner. When a registered worker calls, their row gets a small "Called 9:12 AM" line with a one-tap Present. Unknown numbers go to a short list at the bottom. Fewest screens, and the call sits next to the person it is about.

Unknown numbers: they go to a small "Unknown callers" list at the bottom of the screen, where the supervisor picks a worker or ignores the call.

Recommendation

Use E, with the unknown-caller list from A. A call from a registered worker is the common case, so it should cost one tap, next to that worker's name. A separate tab makes the munshi hunt for it. Unknown numbers are rare and need a bit more thought, so they get their own short list. Two things to confirm: whether workers' numbers are stored (this is personal data under the DPDP Act), and whether the telephony provider passes the caller number reliably.
